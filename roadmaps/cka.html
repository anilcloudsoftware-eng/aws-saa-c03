Certified Kubernetes Administrator Learning Roadmap

A hands-on journey through Kubernetes cluster architecture, deployment, management, and troubleshooting. Build the skills to pass the CKA exam and manage production Kubernetes clusters.

Learning Path

14 Tracks to CKA Certification

Each track focuses on a specific domain of the CKA exam. Start with fundamentals and progress to advanced cluster management. Content is actively being developed.

What is Kubernetes? Architecture overview. Clusters, nodes, control plane. Container runtime. History and evolution of Kubernetes.

Workload Management

Deployments, StatefulSets, DaemonSets. ReplicaSets and Pods. Rolling updates and rollbacks. Job and CronJob management.

Planned

Services & Networking

Service types (ClusterIP, NodePort, LoadBalancer). Endpoints and DNS. NetworkPolicies. Ingress and Ingress Controllers. Service discovery.

Planned
05
🔐

RBAC & Security

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Roles, RoleBindings, ClusterRoles, ClusterRoleBindings. Service accounts. Pod Security Policies and Standards.

Planned

💾

Storage & Persistence

Volumes and persistent volumes. PersistentVolumeClaims. StorageClasses. StatefulSets and data persistence. Backup and restore strategies.

Planned

Configuration Management

ConfigMaps and Secrets. Environment variables. Volume mounts for configuration. Kustomize and templating. Best practices for app configuration.

Planned

Monitoring & Logging

Kubernetes metrics and monitoring. Logs and log aggregation. Prometheus and monitoring tools. Debugging and observability patterns.

Planned
09

Cluster Maintenance

Cluster upgrades and patching. Draining and cordoning nodes. Node management. etcd backup and restore. Component updates.

Planned

Troubleshooting

Debugging pods and containers. Inspecting cluster state. Common issues and solutions. Network troubleshooting. Application debugging techniques.

Planned

Resource Management

Requests and limits. Quality of Service (QoS). Resource quotas and limits. Pod disruption budgets. Capacity planning and scaling.

Planned
12

Cluster Autoscaling

Horizontal Pod Autoscaling (HPA). Vertical Pod Autoscaling (VPA). Cluster Autoscaler. Metrics and triggers for scaling decisions.

Planned

🔄

Scheduling & Affinity

Kubernetes scheduler. Node affinity and pod affinity. Taints and tolerations. Priority and preemption. Advanced scheduling techniques.
