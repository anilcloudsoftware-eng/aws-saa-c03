About This Project

The story behind the AWS Learning Portal — why it was built, what it's trying to achieve, and where it's going.

The Origin

Why this portal exists

This project started with a simple problem: while preparing for the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate exam, most study resources were either too shallow (marketing pages) or too dry (raw documentation). There was no single resource that combined conceptual depth, practical context, and exam relevance in one place.

Rather than keeping notes in a private file, the decision was made to build a proper documentation website from scratch — one that could be shared publicly, grow over time, and eventually help other learners facing the same challenge.

The goal is to write the resource I wish had existed when starting to study AWS. Every service page, architecture diagram, and cheat sheet is written from a learner's perspective — focusing on what actually matters for understanding and passing the exam.

The Principles

How content is written

✓
Depth over brevity Every service page goes deep — not just "what it is" but why it exists, when to use it, and the trade-offs involved.
✓
Exam-aware but not exam-only Content is written to build real understanding. Exam tips are included but they're a by-product, not the goal.
✓
Architecture thinking Services are documented in the context of architecture — how they connect, when to choose them, and real-world patterns.
✓
Living documentation This portal is never "finished." New content is added continuously as knowledge grows.
✓
Always free All content is and will remain free to access, read, and share. No paywalls.
